# REGARD — appointment model site by Kreeative for Jeere

A lash studio (extensions, lash lift, brows) for a **fictional studio**, made as a model for service businesses
on Jeere: white page, black bars, one huge condensed word on the hero, rounded image cards with pill labels.
After the « Lash Lounge » booking-site look. French, prices in FCFA.

Live (unlisted): https://kreeative.xyz/concepts/regard/

## Ground rules

- `noindex, nofollow`, not linked from the portfolio, not in the sitemap.
- Keep the concept bar: REGARD is fictional, bookings made here are trials and nobody is waiting.
- The address, phone, e-mail and Instagram are examples.

## Pages

- `index.html` — hero, « Votre artiste », four service cards, the full menu (« La carte »), the poses,
  hours and address, booking conditions, call to action, footer.
- `reserver/` — the booking steps (service, add-ons, person, day and time, details, confirmation).
- `rendez-vous/` — « my appointment »: the link of the confirmation e-mail (`?rdv=<token>`) shows it, moves it
  or cancels it.
- `mentions-legales/` — legal notice and image rights.
- `site.js` — reveals on scroll only.

## Bookings: Jeere Rendez-vous

Every page loads `https://jeere.kreeative.xyz/connect/jeere-booking.js` with `data-store="regard"`. The script
reads the studio's services, add-ons, team, hours and policies from Jeere (dashboard › Rendez-vous) and:

- fills `[data-jeere-services]` (the menu), `[data-jeere-hours]`, `[data-jeere-policy="…"]`,
  `[data-jeere-address]`, `[data-jeere-intro]`; the page keeps its hard-coded copy if Jeere cannot be reached;
- makes every `[data-jeere-book]` button open the booking, with its service chosen when it names one;
- draws the steps in `[data-jeere-booking]` and « my appointment » in `[data-jeere-appointment]`.

The look of the steps is the script's default stylesheet, tuned at the end of `style.css` (`.jb { --jb-accent… }`).
Free times, prices and deposits are computed by Jeere; nothing in this folder decides them.

The Jeere store `regard` holds 12 services in three categories, 4 add-ons, two people (Maya: extensions,
Inès: brows and lash lift), Monday to Saturday hours with a lunch break, a 5 000 FCFA deposit and automatic
confirmation.

## Duplicate for a client

Copy the folder, replace texts and images, and change `data-store` in every page to the client's store. Her
services, hours and policies are then edited in Jeere, not in these files.

Generated by a script kept outside the repo (`gen_rdv.py`); edit the files directly if it is not at hand.
