# Demo photo credits

The example stores of Jeere show these 69 photos (8 per niche: 6 products, the hero, the about
section; plus 5 for the mock-ups of the landing page). They all come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)
and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, commercial use
included, without asking for permission, and without having to credit anyone. We credit the
photographers anyway, here and in the editor and the dashboard under each photo
(« Photo d’exemple · photographer / Unsplash »). They are not for sale, and they are not the
sellers’ photos: each seller replaces them with their own.

Every photo was resized, cropped and saved as WebP; the few that were also retouched or that only
stand in for what the product name says are noted in italics. A product whose photo did not match
its name was renamed (see « Demo photos » in `../../README.md`); the file names were kept.

Ids in `JEERE_DATA.DEMO_PHOTOS` are `<folder>-<file name>`: `food/hero.webp` is `food-hero`.
