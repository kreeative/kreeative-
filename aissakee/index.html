About

Who is she?

Hi, I’m Kee. I’m a student in Toronto, a self-taught designer and the founder of Kreeative, my design studio. I make UGC that feels like a recommendation from a friend: a real routine, real hands, a real reason to buy.

  • Hook first. I write the first line before I film, so a stranger stops on it.
  • Ready to post. I shoot, edit, caption and design my own content, so you get finished files, not raw clips.
  • English and French. The same script in both languages on request, for Canada, France and Francophone Africa.
  • Honest. I say what I would tell a friend, and I show the product working.
