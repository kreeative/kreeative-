Overview

VANTÉ is a two-day exercise: a landing page for an imagined luxury electric car, built to learn how the big automotive brands sell a car in one scroll. It started dark and Porsche-like and ended light and cinematic, closer to Polestar, with the car breaking out of the hero.

The problem

A car brand’s landing page has to do three things in one screen: set the mood, state the price honestly, and get you to configure or find a dealer. Most imitations get the mood and lose the other two.

Who it’s for

Someone who has seen the car once and is deciding whether to spend ten more minutes: they want the feeling, the monthly number and a button.

Constraints

No framework and no build step. Modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript only.

A `prefers-reduced-motion` path that disables the parallax, the counters and the card tilt.

Two days.

Process

Research

Porsche and Polestar landing pages, for how they balance a cinematic hero with a lease line and two calls to action, and how they handle the scroll into models and specs.

Iterations

Day one: a dark, elegant single-page site with a looping video hero, Archivo and Inter, a champagne-gold accent, reveal animations and a 3D card tilt. Then a redesign as a light Porsche-style theme, and a breakout car in the hero. Day two: the hero reworked into a cinematic dusk landing, then two Figma-ready SVG mockups, one for the hero and one for the whole homepage, so the direction could be edited in Figma before more code.

WHY THIS DECISION

The mockups came after the first build, not before. The code was the fastest way to feel the mood; the SVG mockups are where the layout was settled once the mood was right.

WHY THIS DECISION

A lease line and a starting price under the headline, with a footnote, because a luxury page that hides the number reads as a poster, not a store.

What I designed and built

01 - The hero

A full-screen dusk scene with the car and a three-line headline, the lease and price, two calls to action, and a pause control for the motion.

02 - Models, highlights, specs

A responsive model grid, animated stat counters, and a specs section.

03 - The mockups

Two editable SVG files at 1440px, hero and homepage, ready to drop into Figma.

Key deliverables

  • Landing page concept and copy
  • Light, cinematic visual direction
  • Hero and homepage mockups (SVG, Figma-ready)
  • Static site on GitHub Pages

Outcome

A finished exercise, live on GitHub Pages. It is where the breakout-of-the-frame idea I later used for Guillemot’s plate and ANSE’s bag handles was first tried.
