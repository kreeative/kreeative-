Overview

MICHOU is a streetwear shop for Abidjan: heavy tees, wide jorts and caps, sold in drops. The look comes from street-style editorials: big full-bleed photos, wide black capitals, and everything else set in a typewriter face, lowercase, like a note on a phone.

Like ANSE, it is a model shop for Jeere: a static site where the cart, the order and the payment go through Jeere, so a young brand can take it, put in its own photos and sell.

The problem

Streetwear sells on the picture and on the size. Young brands post drops on Instagram, then answer « dispo en M ? » in DMs one by one. They needed a shop that looks like their feed, lets people choose a size before the cart, and passes that size to the seller with the order.

Who it’s for

Small streetwear brands and resellers who sell in drops and live on Instagram and TikTok.

Their customers, 16 to 30, on a phone, who scroll fast and want the size, the price and delivery in Abidjan in one glance.

Constraints

Every garment comes in sizes, but Jeere products do not have variants yet: the chosen sizes have to travel with the order another way.

Photos of very different origins that must still read as one brand.

The demo images come from Pinterest and belong to their authors and brands, so they are credited on every page and the model must not be published with them.

Process

Type and voice

Wide, heavy capitals for the brand and the section titles, and a monospaced face for everything else, all lowercase. The contrast does the work a logo would do, and it survives any photo.

Sizes on the card

A « + » on each product card opens a small size picker (XS to XL, or one size for caps). The chosen size is kept in the browser and written into the order note at checkout, so the seller sees « T-shirt Lèvres : M » in her Jeere dashboard.

Checkout and mobile

The same Jeere checkout as the other models: commune, delivery fee, mobile money, cash on delivery. I then checked every page at 390 px, which is how almost everyone will see it.

WHY THIS DECISION

Size picked on the card, not on a product page, because on a drop people add three things fast and leave; the size has to come with the click.

WHY THIS DECISION

Sizes sent in the order note rather than faking variants, so the shop works with Jeere today and switches to real variants later without changing the design.

WHY THIS DECISION

A gradient under the titles on the photos, because the shop has to stay readable whatever photo a seller drops in.

What I designed and built

01 - The shop

Home with the drop, new arrivals, categories, the essentials, a lookbook and a moodboard; a shop page with filters; a page per product.

02 - The size picker

On every card and product page, saved in the browser and passed to the order.

03 - The checkout

Delivery by commune, mobile money, cash on delivery, through Jeere.

04 - Credits and legal

Image credits on every page and a legal page on reusing the model.

Key deliverables

  • Art direction, type system and voice
  • Responsive shop: home, shop, product pages, checkout
  • Size picker passed to the order
  • Jeere Connect integration and a Jeere store
  • Image credits and mentions légales

Outcome

MICHOU is live as a demo and is one of the models sellers can choose on Jeere, with orders going through Jeere end to end.
