The Ivory Sukundu

Background

The Ivory Sukundu sells luxury raw hair extensions and glueless half wigs: a comb-in install in sixty seconds, no glue, no lace, ethically sourced and built to last.

The brand needed a home that felt as premium as the hair, an editorial storefront rather than a template shop, with the checkout and order handling that a real store needs from day one.

Core problem

Hair brands online all look the same: crowded product grids, loud discounts, stock photography. The Ivory Sukundu wanted the opposite. Quiet luxury, one hero image, a serif logotype that could sit on a magazine cover, and a shopping flow that never breaks the mood.

Our Process

I designed the identity and the store as one thing. The palette is ivory and espresso, the wordmark is a tall editorial serif, and the photography is given the whole screen.

The storefront is custom coded so the brand controls every pixel, and it hands off to Shopify for checkout, payments and shipping so the business side just works.

What I built

01 - Brand identity

Logotype, palette, type pairing and the tone of voice used across the site, the emails and the packaging.

02 - Editorial storefront

A full-bleed hero, a campaign block for the current edit (“Half the install. All the hair.”), collection and product pages with the install explained in plain words, and an announcement bar for what matters that week.

03 - The Sukundu Circle

A members list with a welcome offer, early access to drops and restocks, wired to the store’s email tool.

04 - Checkout and operations

Shopify checkout with shipping calculated at the last step, customer accounts, abandoned-cart recovery and order emails in the brand’s voice.

Key Deliverables

  • Brand identity and art direction
  • Custom storefront (React) with product and collection pages
  • Shopify checkout, accounts and shipping setup
  • Sukundu Circle sign-up and welcome flow
  • Favicons, social previews and SEO

Results and Impact

The Ivory Sukundu launched with a store that looks like the brand it carries. Every page, from the first scroll to the order confirmation, keeps the same calm, premium feel.
