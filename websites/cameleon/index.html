Overview

Caméléon is a concept for a custom merch brand: USB drives, apparel, accessories, packaging and data services, all decorated with your logo. The name is the promise: merch that takes on your colours.

It is a design proposal for the brand, the catalogue and the ordering flow, built as a working site in Quebec French with an English version.

The problem

Ordering branded merch usually starts with a catalogue and a form, and you only see your logo on the product once the proof comes back. Caméléon shows your brand on the product on the first screen, before you have even asked for a quote.

Who it’s for

Small business owners and event organisers in Quebec ordering a few dozen tees, totes or USB drives, who want to see it before they commit and who write in French.

Agencies ordering for a client, who need a quote they can forward.

Constraints

No backend: the brand studio runs entirely in the browser and nothing is sent or stored; the quote opens a pre-filled email.

Quebec French first (soumission, courriel, coton ouaté), with a complete English version one tap away.

Motion as progressive enhancement: with reduced motion on, or if the script never loads, the page and the studio still work.

WHY THIS DECISION

The brand studio on the first screen, before the catalogue, because seeing your own name on the product is the moment people decide, and it costs nothing to show it.

WHY THIS DECISION

Neutral product photography (black tees on grey brick) so the mint accent and the brand you type are the only things that stand out.

WHY THIS DECISION

A quote in one step through a pre-filled email rather than an account and a cart, because the real purchase is a conversation with a quote, and a form that stores nothing is honest for a concept.

What I designed and built

01 - The brand studio

Type your brand, pick a tee colour and an ink. Your name is printed live on the tee, the tote, the USB drive, the laptop screen and the quote card. It all happens in the browser.

02 - A catalogue in five parts

USB drives, apparel, accessories, packaging and data services, each with its own photo, a short description and a next step.

03 - A quote in one step

The quote form picks up your brand from the studio and opens a pre-filled email. No account, no data stored on a server.

04 - French and English

A language switch that changes every word, the page title and the quote email, and remembers your choice for the next visit.

Key deliverables

  • Concept and page structure
  • Visual direction (palette, type, photography)
  • Live brand preview studio
  • Bilingual copy, Quebec French first
  • Motion design with a reduced-motion fallback
  • Static site, deployed on Vercel

Outcome

Caméléon is a concept, not an official site yet. It is live in French and English, and the brand studio works today: try it with your own brand name.
