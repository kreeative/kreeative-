Overview

Jessica’s Secrets is a concept for a lingerie, swimwear and loungewear brand made for every body. It is a design proposal for the brand’s online store, built as a working one-page site in Quebec French with an English version.

It started from a mockup called Transparency: hot pink panels, a huge italic title crossed by a ribbon, and black and white photos in pink frames. I kept its sections and its boldness, and turned it into a site that works, with real photography, a bag, favourites and a language switch.

The problem

Lingerie is bought on trust: in the fit, in the fabric and in whether the brand has you in mind. The store had to show its pieces on a range of women, mainly Black women and other women of colour, in a range of sizes, and keep every piece one tap from the bag.

Who it’s for

Women in Quebec, many of them Black women and other women of colour, shopping on a phone, who want to see themselves in the photos before they trust a size chart.

The brand owner, who needs to see her mockup become a real page before deciding on a store platform.

Constraints

The client’s mockup set the sections and the look; my job was to make it work, not to redesign it.

No real forms: the bag, favourites and newsletter work in the page but send and store nothing, and checkout is switched off.

Photography mixed free Pexels photos with reference photos supplied for the project, kept off the models’ faces and given one light grade so skin tones stay true.

Quebec French first, English complete, prices in Canadian dollars.

WHY THIS DECISION

“Nouvelle collection” sized to fill the width in either language, with its own phone crop, because the mockup’s whole personality is that title and it could not break on a small screen.

WHY THIS DECISION

Black and white category photos that take their colour back on hover, so the pink frames carry the brand and the product gets the colour when you reach for it.

WHY THIS DECISION

A bag that adds up prices but no checkout, because a proposal should prove the experience without pretending to take money.

What I designed and built

01 - A hero that fills the screen

“Nouvelle collection” is sized to fill the width in French or English, with a pink ribbon unrolling across it. The photo has its own crop for phones.

02 - A bag and favourites

Every product adds to the bag and to your favourites. The bag adds up the prices in Canadian dollars, and checkout is switched off, because this is a concept.

03 - Categories and best sellers

Black and white category photos in pink frames that take their colour back when you point at them, and “Nos chouchous”, a hot pink carousel of best sellers.

04 - French and English

A language switch that changes every word, the prices and the dates, and remembers your choice on your device.

Key deliverables

  • Concept and page structure, from the mockup
  • Visual direction (hot pink, Instrument Serif and Poppins)
  • Photo selection, cut-outs and art direction
  • Bilingual copy, Quebec French first
  • Bag, favourites and newsletter sign-up
  • Motion design with a reduced-motion fallback
  • Static website, hosted on kreeative.xyz

Outcome

Jessica’s Secrets is a concept, not an official store yet. The bag, the favourites and the newsletter sign-up work in the page but send and store nothing, and the reviews are samples written for the concept.
