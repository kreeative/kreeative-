Good to know

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your start date; the remaining 50% is due on launch day. Pay by card (Stripe) or Interac e-transfer. Prices are in CAD and HST (13%) is added.

How long does a website take?

About 4 weeks from deposit, or 2 weeks with the rush add-on. The biggest factor is how quickly I receive your content (text, photos, logo).

Do you also do branding and packaging?

Yes — brand identities, packaging, apparel and visual storytelling are quoted per project. Take a look at the featured projects and book a call to talk through yours.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every build includes post-launch support (30 or 60 days). After that, the Care + SEO plan covers updates and keeps everything running — cancel anytime.
