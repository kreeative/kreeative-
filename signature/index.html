Then in Gmail

  1. Settings (gear) → See all settings → scroll to Signature.
  2. Create new, name it Kreeative.
  3. Click inside the box and paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick Kreeative for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

If the logo still does not show after pasting: in the signature box click the image icon, choose Web address (URL), paste https://kreeative.xyz/assets/img/email/kreeative-mark.png and choose Small.

Profile photo: download the Kreeative avatar and upload it at myaccount.google.com → Personal info → Photo.
