Product designer. Brand designer. Builder.

Hey! I’m Anne-Kelly Kouyaté, a product and brand designer. I design the whole thing: the problem, the people it is for, the flows, the interface, the identity, and then I build it and ship it.

I have been designing since 2019, first in graphic design and content, then brand identities, and since 2024 under my own studio, Kreeative. I also code, so my designs do not stop at the mockup: I design and build web apps, ordering flows, a banking concept and a course platform end to end.

Today I work in Banking CX at Wealthsimple, close to the customers whose problems product teams solve. That is where every design starts for me: what people are actually trying to do, what goes wrong, and what the data says.

Outside of that, I’m into fashion, learning in public and building my own products, some live, some still in progress.
