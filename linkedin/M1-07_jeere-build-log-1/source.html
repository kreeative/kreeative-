Finding 01

They already sell on WhatsApp.

61% of women-owned sellers on Jumia in Côte d’Ivoire, Kenya and Nigeria also sell on WhatsApp. Source: IFC, Women and E-commerce in Africa, 2021.

What JEERE does about it

Orders and stock in one place, wherever the order comes from.

JEERE · Build log #102 / 07
Kee · Kreeative

Finding 02

Social media is the shop window.

67% of small businesses surveyed promote themselves on social media, and 45% sell online. Source: Africa MSME Pulse, 2023.

What JEERE does about it

A shop link made to be shared, in a bio and in a DM.

JEERE · Build log #103 / 07
Kee · Kreeative

Finding 03

A marketplace does not replace social.

Over 90% of Kenyan micro-businesses that used a marketplace also sold through social media. Source: Mastercard Foundation and Caribou Digital, 2020.

What JEERE does about it

One stock for every channel, so nothing sells twice.

Finding 04

The phone is the computer.

416 million people used mobile internet in Africa in 2024. Source: GSMA, The Mobile Economy Africa 2025.

What JEERE does about it

The whole shop is set up and run from a phone.

Signal is not the barrier. Use is.

Short steps, in French first, that tick themselves.
