## Slide text

### Page 1
- Kicker: Brand identity · Lyzma Industries
- Headline: A hand-lettered identity for a jewellery brand: 6 decisions behind it.

### Page 2
- Kicker: 01 · The brief
- Headline: Handcrafted luxury, without losing the hand.
- Body: Lyzma Industries makes handcrafted, African-inspired jewellery. Too polished and it loses its soul. Too raw and it loses its premium feel. The identity had to live in the middle.

### Page 3
- Kicker: 02 · Lettering
- Headline: Lettered, not typed.
- Body: The jewellery is made by hand, so the name is too. A signature, not a font.

### Page 4
- Kicker: 03 · The mark
- Headline: Circles from textile and beadwork.
- Body: Concentric circles inspired by African craft traditions. Rooted in culture, without being literal.

### Page 5
- Kicker: 04 · Colour
- Headline: One colour: warm gold.
- Body: Gold on white lets the jewellery speak in photos, and it is easy to reproduce on tags and bags.

### Page 6
- Kicker: 05 · Type
- Headline: A script, and a quiet partner.
- Body: The lettered wordmark carries the voice. Everything around it stays quiet, so it never competes.

### Page 7
- Kicker: 06 · Applications
- Headline: Where it lives.
- Body: Shopping bags, tags, stickers that say Hi and Bonjour, and the feed.

### Page 8
