7 UX fixes that get a small business more bookings. No redesign needed.

I audit small business websites for a living. The same seven things come up every time.

2. Hours and location above the fold
3. Online booking, not “call us”
4. Fast on a phone, because that is where they are
5. Real photos, not stock
6. Reviews where people decide
