### M1-06 · Thu Nov 26 · CAROUSEL · EN
My design process in 6 steps, from brief to launch.

```
My design process in 6 steps, with a real project behind each one.

1. Brief: one sentence the client agrees with before I open anything
2. Research: references, competitors, and the questions customers actually ask
3. Moodboard: the feeling, before the screens
4. Concepts: two or three directions, never one
5. Refine: the version that survives being used
6. Launch: live, measured, and written down

Examples inside from Lyzma Industries, Tabouret, The Bakery and The Ivory Sukundu.

The step people skip is number 1. It is the one that saves the other five.
```

### M1-10 · Tue Dec 8 · TEXT · EN
Brand deal lessons.

```
Before I designed for founders, I pitched brands as a creator. The lessons transferred.

What creator work taught me about design clients:
1. Nobody buys “content”. They buy a result they can picture. Same with “a logo”.
2. The pitch that works names the brand’s product, not your follower count. Name the client’s customer, not your skills.
3. Follow up once, kindly. Most yeses came on the second message.
4. Have the media kit ready before they ask. Have the case study ready before they ask.
5. Protect yourself: what is included, how many revisions, when it is paid. Write it down.

I wrote a free guide on landing brand deals a while ago. The same structure now runs my studio proposals.
```

AI and designers.

```
Will AI replace designers? Wrong question. Here is the one I ask.

Which part of my job gets cheaper, and which part gets more valuable?

Cheaper: producing. Variations, code, first drafts, audits.
More valuable: deciding. What to build, for whom, what to leave out, when it is good.

The designers in trouble are the ones whose whole value was producing.
```
