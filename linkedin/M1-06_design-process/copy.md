- Headline: The feeling, before the screens.

- Headline: Two or three directions. Never one.

- Headline: The version that survives being used.

## Caption

```text
My design process in 6 steps, with a real project behind each one.

1. Brief: one sentence the client agrees with before I open anything
2. Research: references, competitors, and the questions customers actually ask
3. Moodboard: the feeling, before the screens
4. Concepts: two or three directions, never one
5. Refine: the version that survives being used
6. Launch: live, measured, and written down

Examples inside from Lyzma Industries, Tabouret, The Bakery and The Ivory Sukundu.

The step people skip is number 1. It is the one that saves the other five.
```
