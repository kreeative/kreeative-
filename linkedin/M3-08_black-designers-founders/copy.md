## Slide text

### Page 1
- Kicker: Black History Month
- Headline: 5 Black designers and founders who shaped how I work.
- Body: Typography only, on purpose. Their work speaks.

### Page 2
- Kicker: 01 of 05
- Headline: Cheryl D. Miller
- Body: Graphic designer and design historian. Her 1985 Pratt thesis on Black designers became “Black Designers Missing in Action”, published by Print in 1987. What I took from their work: Writing down who is missing is design work too.

### Page 3
- Kicker: 02 of 05
- Headline: Emory Douglas
- Body: Artist, Minister of Culture of the Black Panther Party. He shaped the look of The Black Panther newspaper from 1967. AIGA Medal, 2015. What I took from their work: Design can speak for a community, in its own visual language.

### Page 4
- Kicker: 03 of 05
- Headline: Gail Anderson
- Body: Graphic designer, writer and educator. Senior art director at Rolling Stone from 1987 to 2002. AIGA Lifetime Achievement Medal, 2008. What I took from their work: Lettering can carry a whole voice.

### Page 5
- Kicker: 04 of 05
- Headline: Virgil Abloh
- Body: Designer, founder of Off-White. Founded Off-White in 2013. Named artistic director of Louis Vuitton menswear in 2018. What I took from their work: You do not need permission to work across disciplines.

### Page 6
- Kicker: 05 of 05
- Headline: Rebecca Enonchong
- Body: Cameroonian tech entrepreneur. Founded AppsTech in 1999. Chairs ActivSpaces, a tech hub in Buea. What I took from their work: You can build from Africa and be taken seriously everywhere.

### Page 7
- Headline: Follow Kee for design, tech and building in public.
- Body: Save this. kreeative.xyz
