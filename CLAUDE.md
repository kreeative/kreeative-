# Working with Kee on this branch

This branch is dedicated to **Aïssa Kee**, the personal brand of Anne-Kelly
Kouyaté on Instagram (@aissakee), TikTok (@keekyt) and YouTube, and to how it
feeds her studio, Kreeative (kreeative.xyz).

Read `docs/PERSONAL-BRAND.md` before any content, design or planning work. It
holds who Kee is, her numbers, what Kreeative sells and at what price, the
current campaign, the brand look and her preferences. Treat it as the single
source of truth and update it there when facts change.

## Non-negotiables for every deliverable

- Language is per platform. Instagram and YouTube: English only for now
  (French paused there as of 1 October 2026 while Kee decides). TikTok:
  bilingual, with a French hook on screen and spoken, an English body with
  burned-in French captions, and a French punchline at the end. The French
  catches her Francophone audience, the English keeps them. TikTok hashtags
  mix French, English and Ivorian tags. Whenever French comes back on
  Instagram, it gets its own hashtags, not a translation.
- TikTok never gets an Instagram repost. A TikTok video is Kee talking to
  camera about a strong topic (companies behaving badly, petitions, society,
  relationships, faith) or something that helps (creator money, red flags,
  how-tos). The Instagram business content reaches TikTok only through the
  creator-money videos, which carry the funnel to @aissakee.
- Each post gets its own layout. A different font is not a different design.
- Reels and vertical video: keep text inside the Instagram safe zone
  (sides ≥ 90 px, top ≥ 250 px, bottom ≈ 400 px clear, on a 1080 × 1920 frame).
- Export at the highest quality available (4K when possible) and proofread the
  rendered text before handing over: no cut-off letters, no overflow.
- Photos of Kee: never put text over her face, never crop her hair, never
  over-zoom. Skin smoothing only when she asks, and never on a finished look.
- Quote posts: plain solid backgrounds (black, white, pink…), never generated
  images, signed @aissakee.
- Bible verses in modern wording (NKJV style, e.g. "who strengthens me").
- Never use the Christocentrique flyer in promos.
- Anything public describes the outcomes of the paid course, never its paid
  details.
- Links go directly in captions only when asked; otherwise use the ManyChat
  keyword (SPOT, SITE, FLYER, KIT).
- No fake testimonials, numbers or results. Concepts are labelled as concepts.
- Prices come from `docs/PERSONAL-BRAND.md` (same as the live pricing pages),
  in CAD plus HST unless stated in USD.

## Where things go

- `docs/PERSONAL-BRAND.md`: the brief.
- `content/`: captions, scripts, carousels, story sequences and calendars,
  one folder per month. See `content/README.md` for the file layout.
- Decks Kee keeps open while recording are built in her Figma (Figma Slides,
  in her team's drafts) in the brand colours and fonts; the text source for
  each deck stays in `content/` so the repo remains the record.
- Everything else in the repo is the Kreeative studio website. Change it only
  when asked.
