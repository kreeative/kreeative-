# Road to 10K: 5-day launch sprint (Wed Oct 1 to Sun Oct 5, 2026)

English only on Instagram and YouTube for now (French is paused there).
Assumption: the 5-day-old account with 31 followers and 5 posts is Instagram
**@aissakee**. Every video below also goes to YouTube Shorts. TikTok gets its
own bilingual content in `tiktok-oct-1-5.md`, because that audience wants
strong-topic talking videos, not Instagram reposts.

Positioning in one line: *self-taught designer and student who built a studio
from her laptop, showing you how to earn online with creative skills, with real
work, real prices and real numbers.*

Three pillars, in this order of weight this week:

1. **Proof.** Kreeative work, public prices, your TikTok numbers, real receipts
   (only ones you are willing to show, names blurred).
2. **Teach.** How to start: one skill, pricing, the DM, PR packages, media kits.
3. **Motivation.** Faith, the student grind, and the build-in-public challenge.

What this is not: no income promises, no "guaranteed", "passive" or "easy", no
fake screenshots, no course module reveals (outcomes only), no Christocentrique
flyer, concepts labelled as concepts.

## 1. The honest math

- 10K followers in a week means roughly **300K to 1M views on this account**,
  because a new account converts about 1 to 3 follows per 100 viewers even on
  strong "follow for more" content.
- You have three levers, and the plan uses all three at once:
  1. Breakout Reels on the new account (sections 4 to 6).
  2. The audience you already have: **@keekyt** does about 15K TikTok views a
     day (433K in 28 days). The creator-money videos in the TikTok track are
     your biggest lever (section 7).
  3. Collab posts with **@bykreeative**, which put each business video on both
     grids.
- If everything in this file gets executed, 1K to 3K by Sunday is a strong
  result. 10K needs one video to break out. Keep the goal public anyway: the
  challenge itself is the hook, and an honest "I didn't make it, here's what I
  learned" on Sunday performs.
- If the 10K is for a specific unlock (for example TikTok's Creator Rewards at
  10K followers and 100K views in 30 days), say so: that changes which
  platform gets the funnel.

## 5. Filming rules (so you only film twice: today and Friday)

- Batch A today: videos 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 14. Batch B on Friday: 8, 9, 11, 12, 15.
  Updates (4, 7, 10, 13) are filmed each morning in 5 minutes with that day's
  real numbers.
- Two setups per batch (desk with laptop, window or outside) and a change of
  top between them so the grid does not look like one session.
- 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 4K if your phone allows. 15 to 30 seconds. Hook in the
  first second, on screen and spoken.
- Captions on (Edits or CapCut), all text inside the safe zone (90 px sides,
  250 px top, about 400 px clear at the bottom), **never over your face**,
  hair not cropped, no tight zoom.
- Cover: 3 to 5 words, big, pink or black. Each video a different layout.
- End on a line that loops or asks for a comment.
- Proofread every text layer before posting: no cut-off letters.
