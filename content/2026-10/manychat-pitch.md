## Disclosure, every time

- The affiliate link is a material connection: say so clearly, next to the
  link ("affiliate link: I earn a commission if you upgrade"), and "#ad" or
  "affiliate" in captions and Stories. Source: Ad Standards Canada's
  Influencer Marketing Disclosure Guidelines
  (https://adstandards.ca/resources/influencer-marketing/) and the Competition
  Act.
- A paid collab: Instagram's paid-partnership label plus #ad (the packages
  slide promises it).

## TikTok: ManyChat stays on Instagram

- ManyChat's TikTok integration works only with TikTok **Business** accounts
  (not available in the EU, UK or US; Canada is listed as available), and it
  automates DMs people send, not comments: TikTok comment-to-DM is not
  available in North America.
- TikTok's Creator Rewards Program takes **personal** accounts only (plus 10K
  followers and 100K views in 30 days).
- So @keekyt stays personal. On TikTok, the guide is sent to Instagram:
  « Le guide gratuit est sur mon Instagram @aissakee : commente GUIDE sous la
  vidéo épinglée. » That is also the funnel.
