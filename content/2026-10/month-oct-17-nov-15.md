# The month, Oct 17 to Nov 15: Instagram, TikTok and a free live

Built on 15 October from a study of what is working for Jun Yuh (@jun_yuh) and
Abigail Kwame (@muvasclass), with their Reels pulled and watched through vidIQ:
`creator-study-jun-yuh-muvasclass.md`. Every idea copies a move, never their
words, and runs through the rules in `CLAUDE.md`: the viewer test first,
English on Instagram, bilingual TikTok, real numbers only, the course by its
outcomes.

The deck to record from (one idea per slide, same numbers as below): https://claude.ai/artifact/L8Awe94sHWocoS1G2xGMt2

**The rhythm.** Instagram: a Reel every evening at 7 pm, a ready-made post on
Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 12 pm (plus the Sunday verse on Nov 15).
TikTok @keekyt: one video every weekday at 1:30 pm. Kee films every Saturday,
for about four hours: each Saturday films the week ahead, from a run sheet
below. Six Reels need no talking at all (the POV and list format that is
Abigail Kwame's best performer this week).

**The arc.** Week 1, start where you are. Week 2, the system. Week 3, get paid
and announce the free live. Week 4, the live on Sunday Nov 8 at 2 pm Toronto,
then the offer. The month ends on day 45 of 31 → 10K.

## Filming: every Saturday, four hours

Kee sits down every Saturday for about four hours. Each Saturday films the
week ahead, in this order: the Reels at your spot, the Reels that need another
room or the screen, a ten-minute break, the TikToks, the quiet shots, then the
wrap (name the files by number, edit the first ones, schedule). The deck opens
each week with that Saturday's run sheet. Out of time? The Reels come first;
the TikToks move to Sunday. Change your top between videos so the week doesn't
look like one day.

Filmed outside a Saturday, only because it can't be filmed ahead: the five
TikTok topics of the day (T03, T09, T13, T14, T19: ten minutes on your phone
that morning, since news can't be filmed in advance) and Reel 34 (the question
from the live, Monday Nov 9 morning).

offer (Jun Yuh's "comment LAUNCH" event, Abigail Kwame's free masterclass and
