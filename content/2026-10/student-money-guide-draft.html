Stream one · design for small businesses

Start with the thing they need every week

Businesses around you need a flyer every week: a promo, an event, a menu, a hiring post. It is the fastest skill to learn and the easiest first sale.

1

Learn Canva in three days. One template style, one colour palette, one font pair. Good enough beats perfect.

2

Make three concept samples for real businesses near you and label them “concept”. A restaurant, a salon, an event.

3

Send five DMs a day with the matching concept attached. The DM is on the next page.

4

Price it at $95 for the first five, then raise it. That is my public price for an essential flyer.

The DM

Four lines that get replies

Hi [name], I’m Kee, a designer in Toronto.

I noticed [one specific thing about their page].

I made you a quick concept (attached). If you like it, I can have the final ready by [day].

No pressure either way.

  • Specific to them. One real thing about their page, not “I love your brand”.
  • Free value first. The concept is attached, not promised.
  • A date. A date makes it real.
  • No pressure. It is the line people reply to.

Five a day, every day. That is the whole secret.

Stream two · brand gigs and UGC

One page beats 10K followers

Brands pay for content, not for followers. My biggest gig came from a pitch, not from a feed. What gets the yes is one page: the media kit.

What is on the one page

  • Who you are in one line, and the kind of content you make.
  • Your numbers, even small ones, with context: views, engagement, where your audience is.
  • Three content examples that look like what the brand would post.
  • Rates, or “rates on request”, and how to reach you.

Post about the category before you pitch. A brand will look at your last nine posts, and they should already look like the collaboration.

The pitch

Short, specific, one idea

Hi [brand] team, I’m Kee, a content creator in Toronto.

I’ve been using [one product] and I have one idea for it: [the idea in one line].

My audience: [your numbers, with context]. Media kit attached.

Would you be open to a paid collaboration or a product send?

One idea per pitch. One product. One ask. Pitch five brands for every one you hope to hear from, and keep posting about the category in between.

The full version, with where I find brands and how I follow up, is in my free brand-deal guide at kreeative.xyz/teach.

If I started today

The first seven days

1

Days 1 to 3: learn Canva, one template style. Make three concept flyers for three real businesses near you.

2

Day 4: write your one-page media kit, even with small numbers. Put “concept” on every sample.

3

Days 4 to 7: five DMs a day with the four lines, one brand pitch a day. That is twenty DMs and four pitches by Sunday.

4

Sunday: count replies, not likes. Fix the DM, not the logo. Go again Monday.

You do not need followers to start. You need one skill, three samples and five messages a day.
