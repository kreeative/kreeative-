## How to schedule

- **Instagram app:** make the post as usual. On the last screen before Share,
  open More options or Advanced settings and turn on Schedule. If it isn't
  there, check in this order:
  1. The account is professional, Creator or Business: Settings, Account type
     and tools, Switch to professional account.
  2. The account is public.
  3. The app is up to date. Instagram rolls features out gradually, so it can
     appear a few days later.
- **On a computer:** Meta Business Suite, Planner. It schedules Reels, posts,
  carousels and Stories, but only for a professional account linked to a
  Facebook Page.
- **TikTok:** TikTok Studio on a computer has a Schedule option when you
  upload.
- **Until scheduling works:** post by hand with three phone alarms at 8 am,
  12 pm and 7 pm, captions copied from `plan-oct-6-16.md`.

No source says a new account cannot schedule; the usual causes are the four
checks above.

## Every day, whatever happens

- Stories, three to five: a poll, behind the scenes, the reminder of the day's
  keyword.
- Thirty minutes after each post: answer every comment.
- 10 pm: the scoreboard (views, new followers, follows per 1,000 views).
- If you miss a day, skip it. Don't post three at once; carry on with the next
  day's list.
