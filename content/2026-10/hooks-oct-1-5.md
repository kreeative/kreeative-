## The viewer test (run it before anything ships)

If you were the viewer, a stranger scrolling, would you care? Three checks:

1. **Is there something for them in the first line?** A lesson, a number, a
   payoff, a feeling. "Day 2 of my challenge" is about you; "3 videos, 1
   worked, here's the difference" is about them.
2. **Would you stop for a stranger saying this?** Not a friend, not a
   follower. A stranger.
3. **Is it a diary entry or a lesson?** Updates are allowed, but the hook
   carries the lesson, and the update is the proof.

## How a hook works here

1. **Frame 1 has the text.** 5 to 8 words, inside the safe zone, never on
   your face. Most people watch muted.
2. **Say the hook before anything else.** No "hey guys", no intro. Cut the
   first half-second of silence.
3. **Put a specific number or situation in it.** "31 followers", "$95",
   "five DMs a day" beat "how to grow".
4. **One sentence of promise or tension, then a reason to stay.** "And the
   third one is the one nobody does."
5. **Film the hook twice with different first lines.** Post the second as a
   Trial Reel and let the audience pick.

## Delivery checklist (read before every take)

- Eyes on the lens. Energy 20% above normal.
- Hook, then the beats, then the CTA. 15 to 30 seconds, one idea.
- Captions on, text in the safe zone, never on your face, hair in frame, no tight zoom.
- 4K if your phone allows. Export clean, no watermark.
- Two takes of the hook, A and B. Post B as a Trial Reel.
- Proofread every text layer before posting.
