three posts a day at 8 am, 12 pm and 7 pm ET. Twenty are filmed (what you
see, the hook, where to film, what to do while recording); ten are ready-made
posts (carousels, quote posts, one text Reel) that need no camera: the files
are in `posts/`, the captions are below. Instagram first, English only, same
videos to YouTube Shorts; TikTok keeps its own track in `tiktok-oct-1-5.md`.

Decks (same content as this file): the Figma Slides deck
https://www.figma.com/slides/fvEAdMudLnlXHcO7nkWDvj and the web deck
https://claude.ai/artifact/HPtDX1WgWjvmw4oWkvCTGJ.

Three rules, unchanged: the viewer test first (something for the stranger in
the first line), real numbers only (receipts with names and balances hidden,
never the employer's name), and the course described by outcomes only.

Anything in `[brackets]` is yours to fill with the true version.

## The $10K video (idea 2), the beats Kee gave on 2 October

1. Hook: "I'm making $10K this year as a student. $8K is already in, and it
   didn't come from one place. Here's how." ($8K = $7,804 verified plus the
   $830 of gig payouts if they are paid; otherwise say $7.8K.)
2. "I work 20 hours a week at a job." Never say where.
3. "Then I manifested a new job that pays more." Only if that job is real
   and current; if it has not started, "I'm manifesting".
4. "My laptop brings the rest: my shop, design clients, brand gigs."
   Receipts on screen, names and balances hidden.
5. How you can do it: put an email in your bio, choose a niche, start
   creating and build a portfolio (the template is in the free PDF), set your
   price and say it.
6. CTA: comment GUIDE for the free PDF (follow gate on in ManyChat).
7. The course, outcomes only: "the full method to land paid brand deals is my
   course, link in bio." No modules, no price on camera. The brief and
   kreeative.xyz/teach say $59 USD; you said $50: if the price changed, update
   both first. "$2K brand deals" only with a $2K receipt; until then, "paid
   brand deals".
