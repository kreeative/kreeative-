# Nov 16 to 29: @aissakee turns to motivation and manifesting

Kee, 12 November: “I want my personal account Aïssa Kee to be more about motivation, like The Wizard Liz and the people who motivate, manifest things in their life and boost. How to activate the right feelings in life, how to manifest. In English.”

The deck to film from: https://claude.ai/artifact/891755k9vVTz1peJKBWkFh. The study behind it: `creator-study-thewizardliz-motivation.md`. The direction, the pillars and the rules: `docs/PERSONAL-BRAND.md`, “Direction from 12 November”.

Rhythm: a Reel every evening at 7 pm, a ready-made post Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 12 pm, one motivation TikTok a week in the TikTok track (the other weekday TikToks keep their own topics). Instagram and YouTube Shorts in English.

## The five filmed formats

- **Short clip, 7 to 15 s: you walk in or pose, one line on screen, a trending sound**
- **Monologue to camera, 45 to 75 s, one take**
- **Spoken blessing to camera, 25 to 40 s, ends on a comment prompt**
- **Talking head with quick cut-aways, 30 to 45 s**
- **Sunday verse, one slow take, lamp on**

The short clips are the cheap, frequent post (Liz’s most viewed); the monologues earn the saves; the blessings earn the comments. Text always in the top third, above your head, never over your face, and inside the safe zone (sides ≥ 90 px, top ≥ 250 px, bottom ≈ 400 px clear).
